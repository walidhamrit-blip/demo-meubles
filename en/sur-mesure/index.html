Example of a bespoke commission

Method

A bespoke project in five steps

  1. 1

    Brief & site survey

    We start from your plan, your inspirations and your day-to-day constraints. In Lebanon a site survey is carried out on site; abroad, we work from drawings checked with your architect.

    3 to 5 days

  2. 2

    Dessins & calepinage

    Dimensioned elevations, layout plans, stone and timber setting-out. Every execution detail is settled before production starts: that is what guarantees a site with no improvisation.

    1 to 2 weeks

  3. 3

    Prototypes de teinte

    A 30 cm sample board is made in the chosen species and finish, then approved by you or your client. For ceremonial pieces, a full-scale prototype is possible.

    1 semaine

  4. 4

    Brief & site survey

    Cutting, assembly, upholstery, finishing: each trade works in the Tripoli workshop. You receive a photographic progress note halfway through.

    4 to 14 weeks

  5. 5

    Delivery & installation

    Packing in wooden crates, white-glove delivery and installation by our craftsmen. Door, drawer and levelling adjustments are finalised on site, one week after fitting.

    1 to 3 days

Frequently asked questions

Projects, lead times and terms

Do you work with interior architects and decorators?

Yes, it is an important part of our work. We provide the technical files, material schedules, samples and compliance sheets needed to present to the client, and we coordinate directly with the other trades.

What is the budget for a complete bespoke project?

A complete project — woodwork, furniture, stone and lighting — generally ranges between USD 45,000 and USD 250,000 depending on area and materials. A single bespoke piece (table, sideboard, headboard) starts at around USD 1,500. We provide a framing estimate from the first exchange.

Do you work outside Lebanon?

Yes. We deliver and install in Europe, the Gulf and North Africa. Distant projects are managed by a dedicated project manager, with weekly video calls and on-site installation supervision for the largest schemes.

How does payment work for a bespoke project?

A 40% deposit confirms production once the drawings are accepted, 40% is paid halfway through and the balance on delivery, before final installation. Payments are made by bank transfer in dollars, euros or Lebanese pounds.
